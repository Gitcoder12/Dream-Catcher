## How to maintain each company record

For every company, keep these fields separate:

- **Follow evidence:** X, LinkedIn, both, neither, or unknown — only mark a platform when an export or direct user confirmation supports it.
- **Official company identity:** canonical company name, aliases, official domain and verified social handles.
- **Public technical work:** named project/product/research area, official source URL, publication/update date and retrieval date.
- **Teams and roles:** team name, role title, location, level, official job URL, date checked and eligibility notes.
- **Portfolio fit:** repository and the specific skill demonstrated. This is a hypothesis, not a claim of company adoption or endorsement.
- **Application status:** not researched, researching, preparing, applied, interview, closed — never infer status.
- **Evidence quality:** official primary source, reputable secondary source, user-provided, or unverified.

## Verification rules

1. Prefer official company, research, engineering, newsroom and careers pages.
2. Preserve source URLs and dates. A homepage is not evidence for a specific project, team or vacancy.
3. Do not invent employee names, leadership, team membership, job openings, salaries, hiring plans or relationships.
4. Public professional information only. No private contact details, employee dossiers, confidential or classified information.
5. Re-check live roles before applying; listings can close or change.
6. A repository-to-company mapping is a prospective skills-fit hypothesis only.
7. Keep unverified items explicitly unverified; do not silently promote them to confirmed facts.
