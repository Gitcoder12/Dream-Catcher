## Research and integrity rules

- Only public company announcements, official project pages, public leadership pages and named authors/project credits are used.
- People entries are limited to public professional roles. This is not an employee directory.
- No private contact details, personal-life information, leaked material, confidential data, classified data or private org charts are included.
- Company-to-repository matches are hypotheses. They do not imply product usage, endorsement, partnership, hiring interest or a customer relationship.
- Re-check titles, projects and current job postings before applying or contacting anyone.
