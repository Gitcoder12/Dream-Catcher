## Portfolio fit: the four newest AI infrastructure targets

- **Mistral AI:** strongest current links are [ARK](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/ARK) for controlled agent evaluation and safety, [RepoForge](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/RepoForge) for developer tooling and model integration, plus Commander and Resonance. Best fit today: applied AI engineering and evaluation, not frontier-model training.
- **Cerebras:** [Data-Center-Planner](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/Data-Center-Planner), [TokenBurst](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/TokenBurst), [Hardware-Box](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/Hardware-Box) and RepoForge provide adjacent systems, engineering and performance foundations. Direct accelerator/kernel expertise still needs to be demonstrated.
- **Groq:** [Resonance](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/Resonance), TokenBurst, ARK and RepoForge connect to inference evaluation and developer infrastructure. Low-level runtime, compiler and LPU engineering remain substantial gaps.
- **OpenRouter:** Resonance, [AI-Box](https://github.com/Gitcoder12/AI-Box), RepoForge, Commander and ARK align closely with model routing, provider compatibility, usage tracking and developer workflows.

## How to use this list

1. Open the official careers route and confirm a live vacancy, location, level and eligibility.
2. Read the current role description; map its requirements to specific code/tests/papers in the repository matrix.
3. Check the updates feed for the company's current technical direction.
4. Use only public recruiting channels and publicly identified recruiters with source evidence.
5. Treat all repository matches as hypotheses until the actual code and evidence are reviewed.

**Recruiter-data standard:** named recruiter records are added only when a person publicly identifies as recruiting/talent acquisition for the company and the public profile or official source can be checked. The recruiter register is currently empty; the official hiring routes are recorded separately. Groq's dedicated careers route remains unverified. No private contact information is collected.
