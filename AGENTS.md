# Agent Instructions — Dream-Catcher

Read README.md, COMPANY_INTELLIGENCE.md, REPOSITORY_TARGET_MATRIX.md, FEATURE_UPGRADE_PLAN.md and CONTRIBUTING.md before research or editing.

## Research standards
- Prefer official company newsroom, research, leadership, engineering and careers pages.
- Record publication date and date checked separately.
- Distinguish a public announcement from a live product, a research preview, a roadmap and an employment posting.
- Verify current titles before outreach; do not infer reporting lines or hiring interest.
- Mark repository-to-company matches as prospective skills fit, never adoption or endorsement.

## Privacy and safety
- Do not collect private contact details, personal-life facts, private employee dossiers, sensitive personal data, leaks, confidential material or classified information.
- Use only public professional roles and project contributors explicitly credited in public sources.
- Do not scrape restricted sites or bypass access controls.

## Portfolio integrity
- Preserve upstream authorship, licences and contributor instructions for forks.
- Do not claim a company uses, funds, endorses or has requested a portfolio project without direct evidence.
- Keep source URLs, dates and uncertainty attached to each factual claim.
- Human review is required before publishing high-impact people/title changes or application recommendations.

## Agent workflow
1. Identify the exact company/repository and question.
2. Gather official public sources and record dates.
3. Separate observed facts from fit hypotheses.
4. Cite the underlying sources in the file.
5. Update RESEARCH_LOG.md and ACTIVITY_LOG.md.
6. Open a pull request; do not merge, message employees or submit applications autonomously.
