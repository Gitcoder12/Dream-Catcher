See COMPANY_INTELLIGENCE.md for the selected public project updates, public professional leadership/project credits, role families and official sources. See REPOSITORY_TARGET_MATRIX.md for per-repository targets.

## Career proof standard

A company name is not a credential. Each target must be backed by a current official role description and repository evidence: code, tests, benchmarks, research reports or a working demo. Never imply that a company uses, funds, endorses or has requested a portfolio project unless that relationship is real and documented.
