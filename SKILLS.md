# Agent Skills — Dream-Catcher

## Company research
Find current official announcements, research projects, products and career pages. Record publication and check dates.

## Public leadership and contributor attribution
Record only public professional titles and explicit project credits. Verify current status; never infer private org charts.

## Repository-to-role mapping
Map each portfolio repository to plausible technical teams and roles using actual project scope and official role requirements.

## Evidence review
Separate fact, forecast, roadmap, research preview, job requirement and fit hypothesis. Flag stale or contradictory claims.

## Privacy and source quality
Exclude private contact details, personal-life data, sensitive personal information, leaks, confidential information and classified material.

## Portfolio writing
Produce clear summaries for beginners and precise technical notes for engineers and researchers. Preserve attribution and upstream licences.
