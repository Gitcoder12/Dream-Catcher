## Application standard

1. Select a company and a current public job description.
2. Map each required skill to a working repository artifact, test, benchmark or case study.
3. Prepare a concise demo and one-page technical explanation.
4. State exactly what is implemented, simulated, heuristic, experimental or upstream.
5. Use official careers and public professional channels. Do not mass-contact employees or infer private relationships.
6. Do not claim deployment, performance, adoption, accuracy or safety certification without evidence.
